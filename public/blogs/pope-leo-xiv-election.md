# Habemus Papam: Pope Leo XIV

In a historic moment for the Catholic Church, Robert Francis Cardinal Prevost has been elected as the 267th Bishop of Rome, taking the name **Pope Leo XIV**. The announcement was made by Cardinal Protodeacon Dominique Mamberti from the central loggia of Saint Peter's Basilica.

## Key Details About the New Pope:
- **Papal Name**: Leo XIV 
- **Previous Position**: Cardinal of the Holy Roman Church 
- **Religious Order**: Order of Saint Augustine (O.S.A.) 
- **First Words**: "Peace be with all of you!"

## What This Means for Our Parish:
1. We join the universal Church in prayer for our new Holy Father
2. Parishioners are encouraged to learn more about Pope Leo XIV's background
3. Watch for upcoming encyclicals and teachings from the new Pope

Let us pray for Pope Leo XIV as he begins his Petrine ministry to guide the Church in these challenging times.
