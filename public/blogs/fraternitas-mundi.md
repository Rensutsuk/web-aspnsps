# Fraternitas Mundi: A Call to Brotherhood

In his first encyclical, *Fraternitas Mundi* ("Brotherhood of the World"), **Pope Leo XIV** addresses the urgent need for solidarity, compassion, and justice in a world marked by division and conflict. Released on the Solemnity of the Most Holy Trinity, the document calls on the faithful to be “builders of peace and stewards of mercy.”

## Key Themes in the Encyclical:
- **Universal Fraternity**: Overcoming indifference through encounter
- **Care for the Poor**: A renewed commitment to charity and justice
- **Environmental Stewardship**: Echoes of *Laudato Si’*, with stronger language on accountability

## What This Means for Our Parish:
1. Parish ministries are encouraged to reflect on the encyclical during June gatherings  
2. Catechists will integrate themes into upcoming sessions  
3. A parish reading circle will meet to discuss the document weekly

Let us heed the Holy Father’s call to be a Church that listens, serves, and unites.
