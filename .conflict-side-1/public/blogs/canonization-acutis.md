# A Modern Saint: Carlo Acutis to Be Canonized

In a moving announcement, **Pope Leo XIV** declared that **Blessed Carlo Acutis** will be canonized a saint on **October 12, 2025**, at Saint Peter’s Square. Known as the “cyber apostle of the Eucharist,” Carlo's legacy continues to inspire youth and families around the world.

## Why Carlo Acutis Is So Beloved:
- **Age at Death**: 15 years old  
- **Life Mission**: Promoted Eucharistic miracles online  
- **Famous Quote**: "The Eucharist is my highway to heaven."

## What This Means for Our Parish:
1. Youth Ministry will organize a digital retreat inspired by Carlo’s life  
2. Our catechism classes will feature Carlo’s Eucharistic devotion  
3. Watch parties for the canonization will be scheduled at the parish hall

Let us rejoice in the witness of young holiness and strive to follow Christ with renewed zeal.
