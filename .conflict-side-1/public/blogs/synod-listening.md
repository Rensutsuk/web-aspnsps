# Speak, Lord, Your Servant is Listening

Under the leadership of **Pope Leo XIV**, the Vatican has released a new multilingual mobile app called **"Synodia"**, designed to collect prayerful insights and experiences from Catholics around the globe as part of the **Synod on Synodality**. The app encourages users to reflect, record responses, and participate in live global surveys.

## App Highlights:
- **Anonymous Submissions**: Share stories and spiritual insights
- **Global Accessibility**: Available in 12 languages
- **Interactive Features**: Weekly prompts and scripture reflections

## What This Means for Our Parish:
1. Download links and parish walkthroughs will be posted online  
2. A Synod Listening Session is scheduled for June 30  
3. Youth and adult ministries are invited to engage in digital discernment

Let’s join the universal Church in this journey of communion, participation, and mission.
